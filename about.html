About Community Fabric CIC

Who we are

Community Fabric CIC strengthens the social fabric of communities by supporting the people who hold places together.

We back the people who hold places together.

We work with grassroots leaders, community organisers, informal groups and small organisations across the UK: the people running local clubs, residents' groups, recovery projects, environmental action, walking groups, community events, mutual aid and neighbourhood support.

They build trust where trust has been lost. They create connection where people feel isolated. They bring people together across difference, frustration and fear. Community Fabric exists to back them.

Why we exist

Social connection is no longer a soft issue. It is essential community infrastructure.

Across the UK, communities are under growing pressure. People are dealing with rising costs, poor housing, stretched public services, insecure work and a deep sense of being unheard.

This pressure is creating anger, mistrust and division. In many places, people's real frustrations are being turned against other communities rather than towards the systems and decisions shaping their lives.

Strong relationships, trusted local leaders and shared spaces for action help communities withstand pressure, bridge divides and rebuild hope.

What we do

Training, coaching and ethical tech, built around people

Community Fabric provides practical training, coaching, peer support and ethical digital tools to make community action easier, more sustainable and more effective.

We help people build relationships, organise locally, reduce admin, strengthen governance, tell their story and grow their impact, without losing the human connection at the heart of their work.

Meet the Team

The people behind Community Fabric

Jessie Joe Jacobs

Director and Digital Lead

Jessie is a writer, social entrepreneur and community leader with nearly twenty years' experience founding, leading and growing charities, networks and social enterprises. Most recently she was Director of the UK Democracy Network, a national network of around 600 organisations, and co-developed an AI and Democracy research strand with the University of Southampton.

She brings experience in governance, fundraising, business development, digital innovation and organisational leadership, and founded Community Fabric because grassroots leaders need better support, stronger networks and ethical tools.

Anna Joy Rickard

Director and Leadership Development Lead

Anna Joy is a community developer, leadership coach and strategic thinker with over twenty years' experience across international development, government advisory work and local community leadership. She spent eight years as a local councillor and has an MSc in International Project Management.

She brings a strong mix of big-picture thinking and practical delivery, and co-founded Community Fabric because every community has people with the potential to lead, who need the right support, confidence and infrastructure around them.

To strengthen the social fabric of the UK by making community action easier, more sustainable and more effective.
