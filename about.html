About Community Fabric CIC

Who we are

Community Fabric CIC strengthens the social fabric of communities by supporting the people who hold places together.

We work with grassroots leaders, community organisers, informal groups and small organisations across the UK. These are the people running local clubs, residents' groups, recovery projects, environmental action, walking groups, community events, mutual aid and neighbourhood support.

They build trust where trust has been lost. They create connection where people feel isolated. They bring people together across difference, frustration and fear.

Community Fabric exists to back them.

Why we exist

Across the UK, communities are under growing pressure. People are dealing with rising costs, poor housing, stretched public services, insecure work and a deep sense of being unheard.

This pressure is creating anger, mistrust and division. In many places, people's real frustrations are being turned against other communities rather than towards the systems and decisions shaping their lives. Neighbours are becoming strangers. Public debate feels harsher. Many people feel less hopeful about the future and less connected to the people around them.

At the same time, local voluntary groups and informal community leaders are being asked to carry more responsibility with fewer resources. Many face burnout, isolation, bureaucracy and a lack of practical support.

We believe social connection is no longer a soft issue. It is essential community infrastructure. Strong relationships, trusted local leaders and shared spaces for action help communities withstand pressure, bridge divides and rebuild hope.

What we do

Community Fabric provides practical training, coaching, peer support and ethical digital tools to make community action easier, more sustainable and more effective.

We help people build relationships, organise locally, reduce admin, strengthen governance, tell their story, access support and grow their impact without losing the human connection at the heart of their work.

Our work brings together place-based community development and ethical technology. We believe digital tools should support connection, widen participation and reduce barriers to action. Technology should help people find each other, organise together and strengthen local trust.

Our support

We support grassroots leaders, community organisers, informal groups, volunteers and small organisations working to bring people together, bridge division and strengthen the places they live through:

  • Community organising and resilience training — helping people listen deeply, build relationships across difference and act together around shared concerns.
  • Leadership coaching and peer support — giving community leaders space to reflect, solve problems and avoid burnout.
  • Governance and infrastructure guidance — helping groups build safe, trusted and sustainable local activity.
  • Ethical digital tool development — designed with communities to reduce admin, support fundraising, improve visibility and make organising easier.
  • Digital skills training — helping volunteers and leaders use online tools, AI, data and communication systems with confidence and care.

The change we want to see

We want a future where community life thrives, even in difficult times.

A future where people feel less isolated and more able to shape the places they live. Where local leaders are recognised and resourced. Where small acts of connection are valued and where communities have the relationships, tools and confidence to resist division and act together.

By investing in the people who hold communities together, Community Fabric helps build stronger local leadership, deeper belonging and more resilient communities.

Our mission is simple: to strengthen the social fabric of the UK by making community action easier, more sustainable and more effective.

Meet the Team

The people behind Community Fabric

Jessie Joe Jacobs

Director and Digital Lead

Jessie is a writer, social entrepreneur and community leader with nearly twenty years' experience founding, leading and growing charities, networks and social enterprises.

She has worked across civil society, democracy, community campaigning and ethical technology. Most recently, Jessie was Director of the UK Democracy Network, a national network of around 600 organisations, where she helped develop work on power, voice, local participation and democratic renewal. She also co-developed an AI and Democracy research strand with the University of Southampton.

Jessie brings experience in governance, fundraising, business development, digital innovation and organisational leadership. She founded Community Fabric because she believes grassroots leaders need better support, stronger networks and ethical tools to help them hold communities together.

Anna Joy Rickard

Director and Leadership Development Lead

Anna Joy is a community developer, leadership coach and strategic thinker with over twenty years' experience across international development, government advisory work and local community leadership.

She has worked with charities, social enterprises and community organisations on leadership, strategy, delivery and organisational change. She also spent eight years as a local councillor and has an MSc in International Project Management.

Anna Joy brings a strong mix of big-picture thinking and practical delivery. She understands the pressures community leaders face and helps people move from ambition to action. She co-founded Community Fabric because she believes every community has people with the potential to lead, and they need the right support, confidence and infrastructure around them.

JP

John Page

Director and Community Organising Lead

John is an experienced community organiser with decades of practical experience supporting grassroots leaders, campaigners, community groups and social movements.

He trained through the Ella Baker School of Organising and has worked with organisations including HOPE not hate, the Jo Cox Foundation, the Equality Trust and the Runnymede Trust. His work has focused on community cohesion, racial and economic justice, relationship-building and organising in places facing division and disconnection.

John brings deep experience in listening, organising and building trust across communities. He joined Community Fabric because he believes the next generation of grassroots leaders needs strong training, peer support, coaching and digital tools to help them build power and create lasting change.
