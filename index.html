About Fabric

Who We Are

Fabric exists to strengthen social fabric by supporting local changemakers, nurturing everyday acts of connection, and using ethical technology at its best to make community action easier and longer-lasting.

We're three people who believe deeply in the power of community. Each of us brings our own story, our own work in the world, and a shared commitment to making connection easier and more meaningful.
